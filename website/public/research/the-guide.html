Architecting Emotional Intelligence in AI

Training an AI to simulate human emotion requires moving beyond factual correctness. It demands Reinforcement Learning from Emotional Feedback (RLEF) and mapping psychological states into multi-dimensional vector spaces.

Plutchik's Wheel

While VAD is a continuous vector space, Plutchik categorizes emotions distinctly. It's used in parallel to classify conversational intent for precise AI routing.

8 Primaries

Joy vs. Sadness, Anger vs. Fear, Trust vs. Disgust, and Surprise vs. Anticipation.

Intensity Mapping

Emotions intensify toward the core. (e.g., Annoyance → Anger → Rage). Used to prevent escalation.
