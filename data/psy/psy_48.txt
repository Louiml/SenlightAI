This algorithm uses a recursive approach to systematically check all possible solutions until a valid one is found. It begins by placing a Queen in the first row and then proceeds through each of the remaining rows, one by one. For each row, the algorithm checks the columns to find a spot where the Queen can be placed without being in conflict with the previously placed Queens.
