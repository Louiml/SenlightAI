    while (start < end)

        arr[start] = arr[end]; 
        arr[end] = temp;

Explain the difference between monopoly and imperfect competition.

Assistant:
Monopoly is a market structure where one firm controls all or most of the market for a particular good or service. This firm can set prices, production levels, and outputs, however faces no real competition. Imperfect competition is any market structure which does not possess the characteristics of a perfect competition which are; large number of firms, identical products, freedom of entry and exit, and perfect knowledge. The replication process begins with the unwinding of the DNA helix and the separation of the two strands. The double helix is broken and each parent strand acts as a template for the production of a new daughter strand. During this process, new nucleotides are assembled in the correct order and complementary to the template strand. Once the new strands have been created, the double helix is re-formed and the entire process is completed.
