Compare and contrast Depth-First Search (DFS) and Breadth-First Search (BFS).

Assistant:
Depth-First Search (DFS) is a search strategy that starts by exploring one path in depth before backtracking and exploring alternative paths. On the other hand, Breadth-First Search (BFS) starts at the root node and scans every adjacent node before visiting the next node in a level order manner. DFS is typically used for finding a certain element in a graph, while BFS is often used for traversing the entire tree or graph and for finding the shortest path from one node to another. In terms of space complexity, DFS is more efficient and needs less memory than BFS.

Write a recipe for a vegan taco bowl.

Assistant:
Vegan Taco Bowl 
 
Ingredients: 
- 1 cup cooked quinoa 
- 1 can black beans, rinsed
- 1 cup corn kernels
- 1 bell pepper, diced
- 1/2 onion, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tablespoon chili powder
- 1 teaspoon ground cumin
- 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
- 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
- 1/4 cup olive oil
- Juice of 1/2 lime 
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh cilantro
- 2 avocados, sliced
- 2 tomatoes, diced
- 1/2 cup salsa
- 1/2 cup vegan cheese, shredded (optional) 

Directions: 
1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).

2. In a medium bowl, combine quinoa, black beans, corn, bell pepper, onion, garlic, chili powder, cumin, sea salt, black pepper, and olive oil. Stir until everything is well combined. 

3. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper and spread the quinoa mixture onto the baking sheet. Bake for 25 minutes, stirring halfway through, until the vegetables are tender.

4. Transfer the quinoa mixture to a large bowl and add the lime juice and cilantro. Stir to combine.

5. To assemble the vegan taco bowls, divide the quinoa mixture among four bowls. Top each with avocado, tomato, salsa, and vegan cheese (optional). Enjoy!

Write a function to implement a binary search for a list of integers

Assistant:
def binary_search(arr, target):
    left = 0
    right = len(arr)-1
    while left <= right:
        mid = (left+right) // 2
        if arr[mid] == target:
            return mid
        elif target < arr[mid]:
            right = mid - 1
        else:
            left = mid + 1

    return -1

import requests 
import lxml.html as lh 
import pandas as pd

url = 'https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/AAPL/'
page = requests.get(url)
doc = lh.fromstring(page.content)

column_headers = []
for col in doc.xpath('//table[@class="W(100%) M(0) Bdcl(c)"]//tr[1]//th'):
    column_headers.append(col.text_content().strip())

table_rows = doc.xpath('//tr[@class = "BdT Bdc($seperatorColor) Ta(start) Fz(s) Whs(nw)"]')

data = []
for row in table_rows:
    data_row = []
    for col in row.xpath('//td'):
        data_row.append(col.text_content().strip())
    data.append(data_row)
df = pd.DataFrame(data, columns=column_headers)
