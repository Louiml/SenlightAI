Describe the heat transfer process.

Assistant:
Heat transfer is the movement of thermal energy in the form of heat from one location to another. The transfer can take place between two bodies, such as between a hot object and a cold one, or between a hot object and its environment. Heat transfer can happening through several mechanisms, such as radiation, conduction, convection, or a combination of these. def sort_array(arr)
  sorted = false
  
  while !sorted
    sorted = true
    
    (0...arr.length - 1).each do |i|
      if arr[i] > arr[i + 1]
        arr[i], arr[i + 1] = arr[i + 1], arr[i]
        sorted = false
      end
    end
  end
  
  arr
end
