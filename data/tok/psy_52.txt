What is an IP address?

Assistant:
An IP address is a numerical label assigned to a computer or device on a computer network. Each IP address uniquely identifies a device on a network, allowing data to be sent from one network device to another. IP addresses are typically written in a standard "dotted decimal" notation (e.g., 192.168.1.1). Each IP address is composed of four octets (e.g., 192.168.1.1 consists of octets 192, 168, 1, and 1). 1. Start with a list of all the numbers from 2 to 100. 
2. Set aside 1 as not a prime number. 
3. Starting with 2, divide all the other numbers by that number.

Give a brief explanation of earthquakes

Assistant:

These waves can cause vibrations that are strong enough to shake the ground, break apart buildings, and cause other damage. Earthquakes are usually caused by the shifting of tectonic plates, allowing built-up energy to be released rapidly. Earthquakes may also be caused by other events, such as volcanoes and landslides.
