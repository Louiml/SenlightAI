Assistant:
Ingredients:
- 1 ½ cups of graham cracker crumbs
- 4 tablespoons of butter, melted
- 16 ounces of cream cheese, softened
- 2/3 cup white sugar
- 2 eggs
- 2 teaspoons of vanilla extract
- 3 cups of fresh pitted cherries 
- ¼ teaspoon of salt

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C) and grease an 8 or 9 inch springform pan.

2. In a medium bowl, mix together the graham cracker crumbs and melted butter. Press the mixture onto the bottom and up the sides of the pan.

3. In a large bowl, beat the cream cheese and sugar until smooth. Beat in the eggs, one at a time, then stir in the vanilla.

4. Pour the cream cheese mixture evenly into the graham cracker crust.

5. Scatter the cherries over the top of the cheesecake.

6. Bake in preheated oven for 45 minutes. Let cool before slicing and serving. Enjoy!

Explain why this algorithm works.

User: def binary_search(list, target):
  lower = 0 
  upper = len(list) - 1
    
  while lower <= upper: 
    mid = (lower + upper) // 2  # Floor Division
    guess = list[mid]
    if guess == target:
      return mid
    if guess > target:
      upper = mid - 1
    else: 
      lower = mid + 1
 
  return None

Assistant:
This algorithm works by using a divide and conquer approach. It begins by dividing the given list into two parts and finds a tuned position by repeatedly halving the list until the target element is found. This approach is advantageous because it greatly reduces the number of comparisons required to find the target value. If the element is not found, upper and lower bounds for the search become equal, meaning the element does not exist in the list. Hence, the algorithm is guaranteed to find the target (if it exists) or return None if it does not exist.
